EN: If the result is different from your usual beer, that is the point. This quiz is about meeting styles you have not tried yet but might love. Go and try one at the festival.

### ui
JA: L/F＝飲み口（軽い／重い）・S/B＝苦み（甘め／苦め）・A/C＝香り（華やか／控えめ）・H/M＝原料（ホップ／モルト）。タップするとそのタイプを見られます。
EN: L/F = body (light/full), S/B = bitterness (sweet/bitter), A/C = aroma (bold/subtle), H/M = base (hop/malt). Tap a tile to see that type.
場所: p
